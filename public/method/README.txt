Method page photos
==================
Upload four photos into this folder with exactly these names. Until a photo is
uploaded, the page shows a soft placeholder in its place.

  hero.jpg     Researcher or scientist examining a supplement label or capsules in a lab
  panel.jpg    Dietitian or doctor at a desk taking notes, comparing product labels
  reviews.jpg  Person at home opening a delivered package or holding a supplement bottle
  retail.jpg   Supplement bottles on a store or pharmacy shelf

Free, commercial-use photos: unsplash.com or pexels.com (search the phrases above).
Landscape photos around 1600 px wide work best. Keep each file under 500 KB
(squoosh.app compresses them for free). Avoid photos showing a real brand's label.
